Cap Labs is developing CAP, a stablecoin protocol to outsource yield generation in a programmatically secured manner. The protocol issues cUSD, a stablecoin backed 1:1 by USDC/USDT, and coordinates three key actors: minters (stablecoin holders), operators (institutions like HFT firms and DeFi protocols that generate yield), and restakers (who provide shared security through restaked ETH). Built on shared security marketplaces like EigenLayer, CAP enables scalable yield generation regardless of market conditions. For more information, visit [Cap Labs](https://cap.app/).
